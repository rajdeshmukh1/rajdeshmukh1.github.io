<p class="intro-lead">
Hello! I'm a software engineer on the <strong>Perception R&amp;D</strong> team at <a href="https://plus.ai/">PlusAI</a> in the SF Bay Area, California. I focus on the design and implementation of perception modules — specifically <strong>lane detection and tracking</strong> — for the software-based autonomy that powers their <a href="https://www.plus.ai/solutions/superdrive">SuperDrive</a> deliverables.
</p>

Prior to this, I was part of the Object Fusion team at [Aptiv](https://www.aptiv.com/en/solutions/adas), a vital component of their Active Safety product. I earned my Ph.D. and master's in the [FD&C/HSL](https://sites.google.com/view/fdchsl/home) at Purdue University, and my bachelor's at [IIT Kharagpur](http://www.ae.iitkgp.ac.in/). Throughout all my degrees I majored in aerospace/aeronautical engineering, with a focus on **state estimation**, **control theory**, and **applied machine learning**.
